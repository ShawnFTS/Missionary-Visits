## Use case summary (30497)
Missionary Visits is a free volunteer scheduling service built and operated by FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC for
members of the Eagle Mountain West Stake (a local congregation group). Families sign up on our website to host the
full-time missionaries for a short visit. Text messages are sent only to people who check an unchecked consent box on our
website, and only about their own visit. There are two message types: (1) appointment reminders for a visit they booked
(up to 2 per visit: 1 day before and 1 hour before); (2) wait-list notices, sent only to people who joined a wait-list for
a full week, when a visit in that week cancels. A small number of operational texts (visit booked or cancelled) also go to
the volunteer missionaries' own phone number, which the site administrator enters. We send no marketing or promotional
messages. Every message identifies "Missionary Visits", and recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
Mobile numbers and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

## Opt-in workflow description (30506)
On https://www.missionaryvisits.com a visitor selects a visit time and enters their family name and mobile number. To
receive reminders they tick the unchecked "Text me reminders" box, choose 1 day and/or 1 hour before, and read the notice
beside the box: "By checking a box you agree to receive automated text messages from Missionary Visits (operated by FIDUS
Technology Solutions LLC) about this visit only: up to 2 messages per visit. Consent is not a condition of signing up.
Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help." with links to the Terms and Privacy Policy.
For wait-lists, a visitor must tick a required, unchecked box with equivalent wording before they can join. Numbers are
never added without this action. The full flow with screenshots is documented at
https://www.missionaryvisits.com/sms-opt-in.
