Eagle Mountain West Stake Missionary Member Visits

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Missionary Visits is a free volunteer service operated by FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC for members of the Eagle Mountain West Stake, so that families can sign up to host the full-time missionaries for a short visit. It is not an official website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Information we collect

  • Your family name and mobile phone number, which you enter when you sign up for a visit or join a wait-list.
  • An address, only if you choose to enter one, so the missionaries can find your home.
  • The visit time you choose, and whether you asked for text reminders.
  • Anonymous visit counts: a random cookie and the type of device and browser, used to see how many people use this page. This does not include your name, phone number, address or IP address.

How we use it

  • To schedule your visit and show the booked time (with your family name only) on the sign-up page.
  • To give the missionaries what they need to visit you: your family name, phone number and address, on a private page and calendar that only they can open.
  • If you check a box to receive texts: to send the text messages described below.

Your phone number and address are never shown on the public sign-up page.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the missionaries who will visit you and with our text-message provider, Twilio, which delivers the messages for us. We do not sell your information. We do not share your mobile phone number or your text-message opt-in or consent with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messages

We send text messages only to people who agree to receive them by checking a consent box on the sign-up form or wait-list form. Consent is not a condition of signing up for a visit. The messages are:

  • Visit reminders for your own visit, up to 2 per visit (1 day before and 1 hour before).
  • Wait-list notices if you joined a wait-list: a message only when a visit cancels in the week you are waiting for.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. See how our text messages work and our Terms of Service.

How long we keep it

We keep visit records for the length of the sign-up schedule so the missionaries and organizers can see who is visiting. A wait-list entry stops being used as soon as you book a visit, leave the wait-list, or an organizer removes it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see Contact us).

Your choices

  • Cancel a visit at any time with the private link on your confirmation page.
  • Leave a wait-list with the link in your wait-list page.
  • Stop texts by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to delete your information by contacting us below.

Children

This service is for adults who are signing up their household. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
