The service

You can choose a visit time, enter your family name, mobile number and an optional address, and join a wait-list when a week is full. A booking is a request to host. Visits may be rescheduled or cancelled by the stake or the missionaries, and we do not guarantee any particular visit.

Text messaging program

Program name: Missionary Visits text messages. Sender: FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC.

  • What you will receive: automated text messages about your own visit only — reminders for a visit you booked (up to 2 per visit: 1 day before and 1 hour before), and, if you joined a wait-list, a message when a visit cancels in the week you are waiting for.
  • How you agree: by checking the text-message consent box on the sign-up form or wait-list form. Consent is not a condition of signing up for a visit or joining a wait-list.
  • How often: message frequency varies with your choices; typically 2 messages per visit, plus a wait-list message only if a visit cancels.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, set by your mobile carrier.
  • Stop: reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will get one confirmation and no more messages.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact us at 801-404-4111.
  • Carriers: supported on major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • We do not share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
