Eagle Mountain West Stake Missionary Member Visits

Text messaging program

Missionary Visits is a free volunteer service operated by FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC for members of the Eagle Mountain West Stake, so that families can sign up to host the full-time missionaries for a short visit. It is not an official website of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Who gets texts, and how they agree

Text messages are sent only to people who check a consent box on a form on this website. Nobody is added from a list, and consent is not a condition of signing up for a visit. There are two opt-in forms:

1. Visit reminders (on the sign-up form)

After choosing a visit time, a person ticks Text me reminders, chooses 1 day before and/or 1 hour before, and reads this notice next to the box:

By checking a box you agree to receive automated text messages from Missionary Visits (operated by FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC) about this visit only: up to 2 messages per visit. Consent is not a condition of signing up. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

2. Wait-list notices (on the wait-list form)

When a week is full, a person can join its wait-list. They must tick this required box before joining:

By checking this box you agree to receive automated text messages from Missionary Visits (operated by FIDUS Technology Solutions LLC) when a visit time opens up in the week you are waiting for. You get a message only when a visit cancels. Consent is not a condition of signing up for a visit. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Sample messages

  • Reminder: “Missionary Visits reminder: Smith Family, Thursday, Oct 8 at 7:30 PM. Manage or cancel: https://www.missionaryvisits.com/b/… Reply STOP to opt out.”
  • Wait-list: “Missionary Visits: a visit time opened up in Springwater Ward's week: Thu, Oct 8 7:30 PM. You have first pick for the next 30 minutes. https://www.missionaryvisits.com/w/… Reply STOP to opt out.”

The volunteer missionaries' own phone, entered by the site administrator, also receives short operational texts when a visit is booked or cancelled. These go to the service's own volunteers, not to the public.

Message frequency, cost, stopping and help

  • Frequency: up to 2 reminders per visit, plus a wait-list message only when a visit cancels in the week someone is waiting for.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Stop: reply STOP at any time. Help: reply HELP or call 801-404-4111.
  • We never share mobile numbers or text consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
