Missionary page & calendar

The missionary link is off. In Railway → Variables, add MISSIONARY_KEY set to a long random value (letters and numbers), then redeploy and reload this page.

Give the missionaries one link. It opens a private page listing every upcoming visit with the family's phone number and address (tap the address for directions), and lets them download visits or subscribe so every new sign-up and cancellation appears on their calendar by itself.

Treat it like a password. Anyone with the link can see families' phone numbers and addresses. If it leaks, change MISSIONARY_KEY in Railway and the old link stops working.

Subscribe on iPhone / Mac

How the missionaries subscribe

  1. iPhone: open their page and tap Subscribe, or Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste the feed link.
  2. Android / Google Calendar: on a computer, calendar.google.com → Other calendars (+) → From URL → paste the feed link → Add.
  3. Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste.

Calendars refresh on their own schedule: iPhone about hourly, Google can take several hours up to a day. For a visit today or tomorrow, a text still beats waiting on the calendar.

Message you can send them

Who gets texted

The missionaries' phone, the number shown at the bottom of the sign-up page, is texted right away when a visit is cancelled, with the family's name, time and phone number. The time then opens up on the sign-up page for another family.

To change that number, use Settings → Contact info on the page.

What these numbers mean

  • A "visitor" is a browser, not a person: one anonymous cookie each. A family opening it on a phone and a laptop counts twice; a private window counts as new; opening the link inside Messages and again in Safari can count twice.
  • Refreshing within 30 minutes isn't counted again. Link-preview robots and most scanners are filtered out, so numbers run a little low rather than high.
  • No names, phone numbers, addresses or IP addresses are stored with visits, and "signed up" is not tied to which family.
  • Tag your links to see where visitors come from: share /?src=harmony in one ward's group and /?src=springwater in another, and they appear under "Tagged links".
  • Don't count yourself: open /?notrack once on each of your own devices.
