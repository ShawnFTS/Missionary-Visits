Missionary calendar

Give the missionaries this link once. Every visit — including new sign-ups and cancellations — then appears on their phones by itself, with the family's phone number and address in the event.

Treat it like a password. Anyone with the link can see families' phone numbers and addresses. If it leaks, change ADMIN_PASSWORD in Railway and the old link stops working.

Open in calendar app (iPhone / Mac)

How the missionaries subscribe

  1. iPhone: tap the link above, or Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste it.
  2. Android / Google Calendar: on a computer, open calendar.google.com → Other calendars (+) → From URL → paste → Add. It then shows on their phone.
  3. Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste.

Calendars refresh on their own schedule: iPhone about hourly, Google can take several hours up to a day. For a visit that is today or tomorrow, a text still beats waiting on the calendar.

What these numbers mean

  • A "visitor" is a browser, not a person: one anonymous cookie each. A family opening it on a phone and a laptop counts twice; a private window counts as new; opening the link inside Messages and again in Safari can count twice.
  • Refreshing within 30 minutes isn't counted again. Link-preview robots and most scanners are filtered out, so numbers run a little low rather than high.
  • No names, phone numbers, addresses or IP addresses are stored with visits, and "signed up" is not tied to which family.
  • Tag your links to see where visitors come from: share /?src=harmony in one ward's group and /?src=springwater in another, and they appear under "Tagged links".
  • Don't count yourself: open /?notrack once on each of your own devices.
